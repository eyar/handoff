Accessibility Statement

WCAG 2.2 AA Last Updated: September 27, 2026
Our Commitment: Handoff is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all users, regardless of ability or assistive technology. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards, striving to meet or exceed WCAG 2.2 Level AA guidelines.

1. Conformance Status

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) define requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA.

Handoff targets and adheres to WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance across all core web workflows. Our digital interfaces are designed to comply with global accessibility frameworks, including the European Accessibility Act (Directive 2019/882) and Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

2. Key Accessibility Features Implemented

We have engineered specific accessibility features across Handoff, including:

  • Full Keyboard Operability: All core functionalities—including selecting files, uploading, initiating transfers, copying clipboard content, downloading received files, and ending sessions—can be operated entirely using standard keyboard inputs (Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape).
  • High-Contrast Visible Focus Rings: Interactive elements feature high-contrast :focus-visible focus rings with clear offsets (exceeding WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.7 and 2.4.11), ensuring keyboard users always have an unambiguous indicator of active focus. Focus rings only activate during keyboard navigation to avoid distracting pointer users.
  • Enhanced Color Contrast: Text elements, controls, and informational badges meet or exceed the WCAG 1.4.3 minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against their backgrounds. Secondary and muted text is calibrated at 6.3:1 to 7.0:1 contrast for comfortable readability.
  • Semantic Markup & ARIA Announcements: Pages use valid semantic HTML5 landmark regions (<main>, <header>, <nav>, <footer>, <section>). Dynamic real-time events—such as pairing status changes, file upload progress, and bridge notifications—are communicated to screen readers via ARIA live regions (aria-live="polite").
  • Touch Targets & Mobile Navigation: On mobile devices, interactive controls meet or exceed the recommended 44×44 pixel touch target dimensions. Modals support effortless dismissal via backdrop tapping and native browser navigation gestures.

3. Device Pairing & Visual Considerations

Handoff establishes secure, ephemeral sessions between devices by generating a high-contrast vector QR code on the desktop display, which is read using the camera of a mobile device.

Note on Optical Scanning: Because pairing relies on optical camera scanning of the QR code, users with severe visual impairments or hardware without functional optical cameras may experience difficulty completing the visual alignment. We are actively investigating additional accessible, zero-configuration pairing mechanisms. If you or your organization encounter any barrier pairing devices, please contact our support team at contact@handoffdrop.app for personalized assistance.

4. Compatibility with Assistive Technologies & Browsers

Handoff is built with standards-compliant web technologies and is tested for compatibility with:

  • Major modern web browsers: Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge.
  • Operating system screen readers: Apple VoiceOver (macOS / iOS), Android TalkBack, and NVDA / JAWS on Windows.
  • Built-in browser magnification, contrast themes, and OS-level zoom controls up to 200% without loss of content or functionality.

5. Feedback & Contact Information

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of Handoff. If you encounter accessibility barriers, find any feature difficult to use with your assistive technology, or have suggestions for improvement, please let us know:

  • Email: contact@handoffdrop.app
  • Subject Line: Accessibility Assistance / Feedback

We take accessibility feedback seriously and strive to review inquiries and respond in a timely manner, typically within 5 to 7 business days.
