How It Works

1

Tap "Add Shortcut"

When you tap the button above, iOS opens the Shortcuts app and prompts you with Add Shortcut.

2

Share Photos, Files, or Links

In Photos, Safari, or Files, tap the native Share button and choose Handoff to PC.

3

Zero Hassle Auto-Transfer

If your iPhone was already paired with your PC, the file lands on your computer screen instantly. If not paired yet, Handoff opens with the QR code ready to scan!

Shortcut transfers are protected in transit (HTTPS) and deleted within 15 minutes, but they aren't end-to-end encrypted, since Shortcuts can't use Handoff's encryption key. For sensitive files, send them from the Handoff app instead.
