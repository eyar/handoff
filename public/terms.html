2. Description of the Service & Ephemeral Architecture

Handoff provides a lightweight, browser-based relay system enabling users to transfer short text snippets and files between paired devices (e.g., between a mobile phone and a personal computer) without requiring user registration, passwords, or personal account creation.

Strictly Ephemeral Nature & Cryptographic Architecture: The Service is designed from the ground up for real-time, transient relay.

  • Text Transfers: Text messages sent from the Handoff app are encrypted client-side using zero-knowledge 256-bit AES-GCM and broadcasted in-memory between paired devices via encrypted real-time data channels. Text automatically clears from the screen when copied, shared, or after 1 minute of inactivity, and is never written to or stored in any persistent database.
  • File Transfers: Files sent from the Handoff app, of any size up to 1.5 GB, are automatically encrypted client-side using zero-knowledge 256-bit AES-GCM before upload, meaning only ciphertext is staged in temporary cloud storage. The following are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS / TLS 1.3 channels without client-side encryption: text and files sent with the iPhone Shortcut (Apple Shortcuts cannot access your browser's encryption key), and files over 200 MB sent before the receiving device has connected, to a device whose browser cannot decrypt large files on the device (such as iPhones on older iOS versions), or to a device running an outdated version of Handoff. Staged files exist strictly to enable download by the paired receiving device.
  • Automated Deletion: All uploaded files are permanently deleted upon download or sharing by the receiving device, upon room termination, or automatically when they expire after 15 minutes. Scheduled automated cleanup runs regularly to permanently remove expired files from temporary storage.
  • Encryption Keys & User Security: Ephemeral encryption keys are generated locally within your browser and transferred to your paired device exclusively via the QR code and URL hash fragment (#key=...). Handoff servers never receive, log, or store your encryption keys. You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your pairing QR code and session link. Anyone who scans your QR code or accesses your session link with its key fragment has the technical ability to decrypt data transferred within that active session.
  • Not a Cloud Backup: Handoff is not an archival or storage service. You are solely responsible for maintaining original copies of all files and data transferred. We bear no responsibility for lost, expired, or deleted files.
